# Dravidian comparative research notes — 19 November 2026

This note records the research decisions behind the current comparative additions. It deliberately separates cognacy, semantic similarity, modern lexical evidence, and historical attestation.

## ಮನೆ (mane) — DEDR 4776

- Comparative set recorded in the audited `house` entry: Proto-Dravidian `*man-ay`; Tamil `மனை (maṉai)`; Kannada `ಮನೆ (mane)`; Kodava `mane`; Malayalam `mana`; and Tulu `manetana` (household / household life), as reported under DEDR 4776.
- Tulu `ಇಲ್ಲ್ / illu` is a separate Dravidian word for house/home (DEDR 494); it must not be presented as the Tulu reflex of `*man-ay`.
- The audited record now labels the Halegannada/Old Kannada form as `not_yet_verified`. A modern dictionary or contemporary usage does not establish a dated Old Kannada attestation. Add a primary inscription or dated literary citation before changing this status.
- The older compact `data.js` record is supplemented by the audited record; the app merges the audited alternative fields into the base entry.

## ತಗ್ಗು (taggu) — DEDR 3178

- The available scholarly discussion identifies Kannada `taggu` ‘to be lowered, to diminish’ with a reconstruction `*tazunku` (DEDR 3178).
- The same discussion notes Toda `tōy` ‘to be lowered’ in connection with DEDR 3178, but the excerpt does not establish a clean one-to-one cognate set across Tamil, Tulu, and Kannada. This requires checking the complete DEDR entry and the sound correspondences before populating language fields.
- Therefore no Tamil, Tulu, or Proto-Dravidian fields have been added to the public word list for `ತಗ್ಗು`. Do not substitute semantic neighbours for cognates.
- Related words for ‘below / low place’ (e.g. Tamil `kīḻ`, Kannada `kīḷ / keḷa`, Tulu `kīḷu`, and the `paḷḷam / paḷḷa` family) are semantic comparisons, not evidence that they descend from the same root as `taggu`.

## Evidence / source trail

- Burrow & Emeneau, *A Dravidian Etymological Dictionary: Revised Edition* (DEDR), entry 3178; quoted and discussed in the historical phonology study surfaced as “Historical Phonology of Asian Languages” (search result excerpt: Kannada `taggu` ‘to be lowered, to diminish’ < `*tazunku`).
- Krishnamurti, *The Dravidian Languages*, selected lexical correspondences table: DEDR 1619 lists Tamil `kīḻ`, Kannada `kīḷ / keḷa`, and Tulu `kīḷu` for ‘place below’; these are a distinct comparison.
- DEDR 4776 and DEDR 494 are the reference numbers retained in the audited `ಮನೆ` note. The Tulu distinction is explicit to avoid conflating `manetana` with `illu`.
- A primary Old Kannada corpus reference for `ಮನೆ` has not yet been verified; status remains unverified.

## Editorial rule

Only populate a language's comparative field when a lexical form and its relevant sense are supported by a traceable comparative dictionary or scholarly source. Mark missing historical evidence as unverified. Semantic similarity alone is not cognacy.
